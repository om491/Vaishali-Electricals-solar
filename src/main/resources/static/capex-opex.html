What is Capex & Opex?

Two major business models used in solar energy projects.

CAPEX Model

In the CAPEX model, the customer invests in the solar system and owns the complete project. The customer gets maximum electricity savings and long-term returns.

OPEX Model

In the OPEX model, the solar company installs and maintains the system while the customer pays only for electricity usage through a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA).

Smart Solar Investment

Both models help businesses reduce electricity costs, achieve sustainability goals, and use clean renewable energy.
