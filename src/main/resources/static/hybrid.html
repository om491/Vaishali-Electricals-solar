What is Hybrid Solar?

Smart combination of solar, battery, and electricity grid.

Grid + Battery System

Hybrid solar systems work with both battery backup and utility grid connection for maximum reliability.

Power Backup

Provides electricity during power cuts using stored solar battery energy.

Smart Energy Control

Automatically switches between solar, battery, and grid according to power availability.

Main Components

Important equipment used in hybrid solar systems.

Solar Panels

Mono PERC, TOPCon, and Bifacial panels convert sunlight into electricity efficiently.

Hybrid Inverter

Controls solar generation, battery charging, grid synchronization, and backup switching.

Battery Storage

Stores extra solar energy for night-time and power-cut operation.

Net Meter

Measures electricity import and export between system and utility grid.

Protection Devices

Includes ACDB, DCDB, SPD, MCB, and earthing systems for safety.

DG Generator Support

Optional generator support available for industrial and critical applications.
