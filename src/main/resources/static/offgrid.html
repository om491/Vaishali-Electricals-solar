What is Off-Grid Solar?

Complete solar power solution with battery backup.

Independent System

Off-grid solar systems work completely without government electricity supply and are ideal for remote areas.

Battery Backup

Electricity generated during daytime is stored in batteries for night-time and power-cut usage.

Reliable Power

Provides continuous electricity backup for homes, offices, farms, telecom towers, and industries.

Main Components

Important equipment used in off-grid solar systems.

Solar Panels

Convert sunlight into DC electricity using Mono PERC or Polycrystalline technology.

Charge Controller

Controls battery charging and protects batteries from overcharging and voltage fluctuation.

Battery Bank

Stores excess electricity for backup use during night and cloudy weather.

Solar Inverter

Converts DC power into AC electricity for home and commercial appliances.

Protection System

Includes ACDB, DCDB, earthing, and safety devices for system protection.

Mounting Structure

Strong galvanized structure to hold solar panels securely for long life.
