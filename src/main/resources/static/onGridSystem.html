What is On-Grid Solar?

Efficient grid-connected solar power system for modern energy needs.

Solar Panels

Solar panels convert sunlight into DC electricity and are installed on rooftops or open areas for maximum solar generation.

On-Grid Inverter

Converts DC electricity into AC power and synchronizes with the utility grid for seamless power supply.

Net Metering

Extra electricity generated is exported to the grid, helping reduce electricity bills through net metering.

Main Components

Essential equipment used in on-grid solar systems.

Solar Modules

High-efficiency solar panels generate electricity during daytime.

Solar Inverter

Converts generated DC power into usable AC electricity.

Net Meter

Measures imported and exported power from the electricity grid.

ACDB / DCDB

Protection and safe distribution of electrical power.

Mounting Structure

Hot dip galvanized structure for long life and strong support.

Utility Grid

Supplies electricity when solar generation is insufficient.
